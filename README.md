# My Inventory

A very simple stock list with Buy and Sell. Works offline, nothing to install.

## Start
Unzip, then double-click `index.html`.

## How to use
1. Tap **＋ Add item** and type a name and how many you have.
2. Tap **Buy** when you get more stock, or **Sell** when you sell. Type how many and the price for that day. There is no fixed price, so you can use a different price every time.
3. Stock goes up on Buy and down on Sell. You cannot sell more than you have.
4. The top shows how much you spent buying and earned selling.
5. The **History** tab lists every purchase and sale with quantity, price and total.
6. Items turn yellow when low and red when out of stock.
7. **⋯** menu: download items or history (CSV), save or restore a backup.

Data is saved in this browser on this device. Use **⋯ → Save backup** now and then.
